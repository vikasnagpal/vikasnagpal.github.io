OPERATIONAL DISCIPLINE

Four Operating Rules

Every member of the outreach team must follow these four core rules when communicating with potential sponsors:

Rule 01

Sell the story and the assets

Lead with original content and authentic product integration, not generic exposure or charity framing. Sponsors are backing high-production field storytelling in an unrepeatable landscape.

Rule 02

Protect the meaning of the journey

Rinpoche's role is contemplative and cultural, not a product endorsement. Never promise commercial testimonials, scripted product plugs, or brand endorsements from Rinpoche.

Rule 03

Promise only what can be delivered

Confirm talent, accounts, production capacity, rights, and posting commitments before presenting them as guaranteed. Treat talent posts as proposed until mutually approved.

Rule 04

Protect future value

Offer a defined first opportunity on future trips, not perpetual free equity. Negotiate the scope, deliverables, and fees of each subsequent expedition separately.
