01 · THE EXPEDITION CONCEPT

Beyond Conventional Adventure Travel

Upper Mustang is one of the world's last intact bastions of traditional Himalayan civilization. Tucked behind the Annapurna massif in the high-altitude rain shadow, its wind-sculpted sandstone gorges, cliffside monasteries, and ancient walled capital of Lo Manthang have preserved an ancient Tibetan rhythm for centuries.

A Ride Designed Around Focus, Stillness, and Endurance

This is neither a commercial motorcycle rally nor an indoor wellness retreat. Over 13 days and 822 to 3,840 metres of elevation, the team follows the sacred Kali Gandaki riverbed into the barren high desert.

Each morning begins with grounding breathwork and Himalayan yoga before taking to the bikes. The daytime hours are spent tackling technical river crossings, rocky passes, and suspension bridges. In the evenings, the group gathers in quiet Thakali village lodges for unhurried discourses on clarity, detachment, and living with fewer burdens.

The Route: Pokhara → Tatopani Hot Springs → Marpha Apple Orchards → Kagbeni Gateway → Chele River Gorges → Tsarang Ancient Palace → Lo Manthang (3,840m) → Chhoser Cave Gompas → Muktinath Sacred Spire → Pokhara.
