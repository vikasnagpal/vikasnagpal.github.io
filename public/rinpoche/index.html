THE JOURNEY WITHIN

Deep in the Himalayas lies Upper Mustang, an ancient restricted kingdom guided by timeless Tibetan wisdom. For 13 days, travel wind-sculpted valleys and passes up to 3,840 metres alongside HE Palga Rinpoche. Beyond the physical trail, this is a path of inner clarity—where the noise of the modern world falls away, and the silence of the mountains reveals what truly matters.

A sacred crossing through the high desert of the Kali Gandaki.
Where every mile on the road becomes a quiet journey within.

The Way Forward

Over thirteen days, we follow the Kali Gandaki from the subtropical waters of Pokhara into the rain shadow of the Himalayas, climbing towards the high desert of Upper Mustang.

As the landscape changes, so does the rhythm of the journey. Ancient villages, hidden caves and sacred monasteries replace the noise and urgency of the world we leave behind. Each stage is designed to immerse rather than rush, letting the road and its silence become a meditation in themselves.

We reach Lo Manthang at 3,840 metres, then turn south, carrying something of that stillness back with us

Preparation and quiet before the trail.

Free morning to rest or walk the lakeside. Full expedition briefing in the afternoon: permits, high-altitude protocols, bike allocation for riders, and orientation for the 4x4 support vehicles. Early night before the dirt starts.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Full morning is open. Natural anchor for a grounding session at low altitude.

DAY 03 · ALT 2,670 m · NIGHT MARPHA

Pokhara to Marpha

The old salt caravan route.

A long day north into the Kali Gandaki gorge. Pavement ends past Beni; the track turns to riverbed gravel and rocky switchbacks. Arrive by afternoon at Marpha, a whitewashed Thakali village of tight stone alleys, apple orchards, and dry mountain winds.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Evening in the village. Flagstone alleys where wind carries the scent of cedar and dried apples.

FIELD NOTE

“The valley narrows into sheer grey limestone. Firewood stacked two storeys high along whitewashed walls.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Old Salt Route · Kali Gandaki
DAY 03 / 13
DAY 04 · ALT 3,760 m · NIGHT MUKTINATH

Muktinath

Fire and water in the same place.

A short but exposed ride through the wind-scoured Jomsom valley, past the medieval gate of Kagbeni. Climb to Muktinath at 3,760m—one of the most sacred pilgrimage sites in the Himalayas, revered by Hindus and Buddhists alike, where a natural gas flame burns directly above an underground spring.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Evening at a shared sacred site. Natural anchor for a practice or teaching linked to the place.

FIELD NOTE

“108 brass water spouts in the biting wind. The natural flame flickers undisturbed over cold spring water.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Muktinath Temple · 108 Spouts
DAY 04 / 13
DAY 05 · ALT 3,800 m · NIGHT SYANGBOCHE

Into Upper Mustang

The restricted kingdom begins.

Back down through Kagbeni, where the restricted zone begins. The landscape shifts dramatically into ochre badlands, wind-carved canyons, and tiny oases clustered around chortens. Over the Chele pass and switchbacks to Syangboche.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Late afternoon on the pass. Complete silence as the sun drops behind Dhaulagiri.

FIELD NOTE

“The checkpoint gate swings shut behind us. From here on: no phone reception, no modern distraction.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Chele Canyon & Badlands
DAY 05 / 13
DAY 06 · ALT 3,840 m · NIGHT LO MANTHANG

Passes to Lo Manthang

Across the red cliffs of Ghami.

High passes across the Tibetan plateau. Ride past the longest carved Mani wall in Nepal at Ghami, set against blood-red mineral cliffs. Stop at Tsarang's royal fortress palace before descending into the legendary walled capital of Lo Manthang.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Sitting beside the Mani wall in afternoon wind. Turning prayer stones in quiet contemplation.

FIELD NOTE

“Half a kilometre of carved Tibetan syllables. Red cliffs painted with natural mineral ochre.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Ghami Mani Wall · Tsarang Palace
DAY 06 / 13
DAY 07 · ALT 3,840 m · NIGHT LO MANTHANG

Lo Manthang

The walled kingdom untouched by time.

Full day inside the 15th-century fortified capital. Wander narrow earthen alleyways between whitewashed homes. Visit Jampa Lhakhang, housing three-storey golden Buddhas and ancient murals. In the afternoon, a private audience and teaching with HE Palga Rinpoche in the royal monastery courtyard.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Meditation inside Jampa Lhakhang with HE Palga Rinpoche. The spiritual and geographic summit of the journey.

FIELD NOTE

“15th-century butter lamps illuminate 500-year-old mandalas. The king's palace stands quiet in the center of the world.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026

DAY 07 / 13
DAY 08 · ALT 3,840 m · NIGHT LO MANTHANG

Chhoser Sky Caves

Five storeys carved into sheer cliff.

Short day ride north toward the border of Tibet. Explore the mysterious Jhong Cave complex at Chhoser—forty rooms carved five storeys high into vertical rock cliffs 2,500 years ago. Visit Nyphu Cave Gompa, built seamlessly into the canyon stone.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Silence inside the cave chambers. Cool darkness against blinding Himalayan sunlight.

FIELD NOTE

“Ladders carved from tree trunks inside a 2,500-year-old cliff. Looking out over Tibet through arrow slits.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Jhong Caves · 2,500 Years Old
DAY 08 / 13
DAY 09 · ALT 2,980 m · NIGHT CHHUSANG

Luri Cave Gompa

Earthen spires and hidden sanctuaries.

Ride east into the surreal fluted rock pinnacles of the Puyung valley to reach Luri Gompa. This tiny monastery is carved directly into a solitary fluted needle of sandstone, containing 14th-century Mahasiddha frescoes untouched by modern restoration.

SPACE TO PAUSE

The steep foot climb up the sandstone needle. A solitary monk turning a heavy brass key in the wooden lock.

FIELD NOTE

“A dome carved inside an earthen spire. Intricate 14th-century frescoes lit only by filtered daylight.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Luri Gompa · Sandstone Pinnacle
DAY 09 / 13
DAY 10 · ALT 2,800 m · NIGHT KAGBENI

Chhusang to Kagbeni

The confluence of stone and time.

Southward return through the striped cliffs of Tangbe and the red canyon walls of Chhusang. Cross the suspension bridges and rocky river flats to reach Kagbeni, the ancient mud fortress guarding the threshold between Upper and Lower Mustang.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Rooftop tea overlooking the restricted gate. Watching the afternoon dust gale blow across the northern horizon.

FIELD NOTE

“Mud-brick fortress overlooking two holy rivers. Monks crossing the cobblestone square at dusk.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Gateway Fortress · Kagbeni
DAY 10 / 13
DAY 11 · ALT 2,480 m · NIGHT LETE

Kagbeni to Lete

Leaving the desert, entering pine forests.

Descend out of the rain shadow. The desert gives way to alpine pine forests, moss-covered rocks, and mountain streams. Lete sits beneath the towering north face of Dhaulagiri (8,167m) and the Annapurna massif on the opposite side of the valley.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Pine needle trails in the afternoon mist. The body absorbs the return from the high altitude plateau.

FIELD NOTE

“The air turns humid and fragrant with cedar. Two 8,000m giants tower directly above the forest canopy.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Lete Valley · Pine & Icefalls
DAY 11 / 13
DAY 12 · ALT 822 m · NIGHT POKHARA

Lete to Pokhara

Natural hot springs and farewell feast.

Final descent through Ghasa and the deepest canyon in the world. Stop at Tatopani for a soak in the natural geothermal mineral hot springs, washing the Mustang dust away. Reach Pokhara by sunset for a celebratory Thakali feast.

SPACE TO PAUSE

Tatopani mineral soak and evening dinner. A space to share stories from the dust and road.

FIELD NOTE

“Steaming mineral pools relaxing trail-worn muscles. A long table filled with steaming Thakali dishes.”

UPPER MUSTANG EXPEDITION · AUTUMN 2026
Tatopani Springs · Farewell Banquet

DAY 13 · ALT 822 m · NIGHT DEPARTURE

Departure

The journey continues within.

Motorcycle handover over breakfast. Airport and road transfers. The expedition officially concludes, carrying the vast stillness, the altitude, and the shared reflections of Upper Mustang home with each traveler.

SPACE TO PAUSE

A quiet moment of gratitude. A white silk khata blessing before departure.

FIELD NOTE

“The mountains stay behind, but the inner silence travels home with us.”
