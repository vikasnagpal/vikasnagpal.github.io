The Journey Within

From the subtropical lakes of Pokhara to the walled kingdom of Lo Manthang (3,840m), this 13-day expedition is a journey towards the inner frontier. Through the Kali Gandaki gorge, into Nepal's restricted Upper Mustang, and back through ancient trails, meditation caves, and sacred monasteries.

DAY 2

Pokhara: Rest, Briefing, Gear Check

Preparation and quiet before the trail.

Free morning to rest or explore Pokhara (paragliding at Sarangkot, a boat across the lake, or a quiet walk). Full route briefing in the afternoon: permits, riding protocols, bike allocation for riders, and orientation of the 4x4 vehicles for anyone travelling in them. Prep dinner and an early night.

Alt822 m
NightPokhara
SPACE TO PAUSE

Full morning is open. Good day for a longer opening session at low altitude.

Route briefing
Day 3
DAY 3

Pokhara to Marpha

The old salt caravan route.

A long day north into the Kali Gandaki gorge. Roads turn to gravel and riverbed track past Beni. This was the old salt caravan route between Tibet and India. Arrive by afternoon at Marpha, a whitewashed Thakali village of tight flagstone lanes, known for its apple orchards and apple brandy.

Alt2,670 m
NightMarpha
SPACE TO PAUSE

Evening in the village. Quiet flagstone lanes.

Old Salt Route
Day 4
DAY 4

Muktinath

Fire and water in the same place.

A short but exposed stretch through the wind-scoured Jomsom valley, past Kagbeni (the medieval gateway to the restricted zone). Climb up to Muktinath, one of the most sacred sites in the Himalayas: revered by both Hindus and Buddhists, home to 108 water spouts and a natural gas flame burning above a spring.

Alt3,760 m
NightMuktinath
SPACE TO PAUSE

Evening at a shared sacred site. Natural anchor for a practice or teaching linked to the place.

Day 5
DAY 5

Muktinath to Syangboche

Into the restricted zone.

Back down through Kagbeni for the permit check. From here the restricted zone begins. Landscape turns lunar: eroded ochre badlands, wind-carved pillars, and tiny villages clustered around chortens. A technical riding day of switchbacks and gravel. Overnight at Syangboche, a quiet teahouse settlement at the day's high point.

Alt3,800 m
NightSyangboche
SPACE TO PAUSE

Evening. Almost no light pollution; strong night sky.

Restricted Zone
Day 6
DAY 6

Syangboche to Lo Manthang

Arrival at the walled kingdom.

Over Nyi La pass, then through Geling and Ghami (home to the longest mani wall in Nepal) and Tsarang (former royal seat with a ruined five-storey palace). The walled city of Lo Manthang appears suddenly in a bowl of high desert. Park the bikes for three nights.

Alt3,840 m
NightLo Manthang (Night 1 of 3)
SPACE TO PAUSE

Evening. Sunset walk along the walled city ramparts.

Ghami Mani Wall
Day 7
DAY 7

Lo Manthang (Stay Day)

Palaces, monasteries, and ancient murals.

Full day on foot inside the walled city. Visit the Royal Palace, Jampa Lhakhang (the God House with a three-storey Maitreya statue), Thubchen Gompa (assembly hall with 15th-century murals), and Chode Monastery (the working monastery of the city).

Alt3,840 m
NightLo Manthang (Night 2 of 3)
SPACE TO PAUSE

Full afternoon and evening. This is the longest continuous open window of the trip, and a natural place to anchor a longer teaching or shared practice.

Jampa Lhakhang
Day 8
DAY 8

Chhoser Day Ride

Ancient sky caves and hermitages.

Half-day ride out to Chhoser to see the Jhong sky caves (a 50m honeycomb of over 40 hand-carved chambers, purpose unknown, believed to be 2,000 to 3,000 years old) and the nearby Nyphu and Garphu cave gompas, still used by monks for retreat. Back to Lo Manthang for a final evening in the walled city.

Alt3,840 m
NightLo Manthang (Night 3 of 3)
SPACE TO PAUSE

Afternoon and evening. Second full open window in Lo Manthang, builds on Day 7.

Jhong Sky Caves
Day 9
DAY 9

Lo Manthang to Chhusang

Undisturbed 700-year-old cave murals.

Dip south through the Puyung valley, a quieter, less visited corner of Mustang. Leave the bikes at the Ghara trailhead for a short uphill hike to Luri Gompa: the oldest monastery in the region, a cave temple with 700-year-old murals essentially undisturbed. Continue down through Tetang to Chhusang for the night.

Alt2,980 m
NightChhusang
SPACE TO PAUSE

The visit to Luri Gompa itself is quiet. The site does most of the work.

Luri Cave Gompa
Day 10
DAY 10

Chhusang to Kagbeni

Exiting the restricted zone.

Short, easy ride out of the restricted zone. Permits stamped on exit at Kagbeni. Whole afternoon free to explore the medieval alleyways and sit by the river confluence as the afternoon wind picks up.

Alt2,800 m
NightKagbeni
SPACE TO PAUSE

Full afternoon and evening. Medieval village, river confluence.

Medieval Kagbeni
Day 11
DAY 11

Kagbeni to Lete

Descent into oxygen and pine forest.

Back through Jomsom and Marpha, then on into pine and rhododendron forest at Lete. The first proper oxygen-rich stop after eight days at altitude.

Alt2,480 m
NightLete
SPACE TO PAUSE

Afternoon and evening. Good integration day before returning to the city.

Forest of Lete
Day 12
DAY 12

Lete to Pokhara

Natural hot springs and farewell dinner.

Final ride down through Ghasa, where Annapurna I and Dhaulagiri frame the valley from either side, to Tatopani for a soak in the natural hot springs. Back in Pokhara by evening. Closing dinner with the whole group.

Alt822 m
NightPokhara
SPACE TO PAUSE

Closing evening. Hot springs at Tatopani on the way down; celebratory farewell dinner.

Tatopani Springs
Day 13
DAY 13

Departure

The journey continues within.

Bikes handed back over breakfast. Transfers to onward flights or road journeys. The expedition concludes, carrying the stillness and perspective of the Himalayas home.

Alt822 m
NightDeparture
SPACE TO PAUSE

Departure. A quiet moment of gratitude, carrying the stillness of the Himalayas home.
