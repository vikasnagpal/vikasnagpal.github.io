The Journey Within

From the subtropical lakes of Pokhara to the walled kingdom of Lo Manthang (3,840m), this 14-day loop is a journey towards the inner frontier. Through the Kali Gandaki gorge, into Nepal's restricted Upper Mustang, and back through ancient trails, meditation caves, and sacred monasteries.

POKHARA
POKHARA
Day 1

Arrival at Pokhara

The journey begins.

Everyone arrives through the day and checks into the hotel by Phewa Lake. Nothing scheduled until evening. Welcome dinner brings the whole group together for the first time.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 822 m
Night: Pokhara
Space to Pause

Whole afternoon and evening, apart from welcome dinner.

Day 2

Pokhara

Rest, briefing, gear check.

Free morning to rest or explore Pokhara (paragliding at Sarangkot, a boat across the lake, or a quiet walk). Full route briefing in the afternoon: permits, riding protocols, bike allocation for riders, and orientation of the 4x4 vehicles for anyone travelling in them. Prep dinner and an early night.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 822 m
Night: Pokhara
Space to Pause

Full morning is open. Good day for a longer opening session at low altitude.

Day 3

Marpha

The old salt route.

A long day north into the Kali Gandaki gorge. Roads turn to gravel and riverbed track past Beni. This was the old salt caravan route between Tibet and India. Arrive by afternoon at Marpha, a whitewashed Thakali village of tight flagstone lanes, known for its apple orchards and apple brandy.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 2,670 m
Night: Marpha
Space to Pause

Evening in the village.

Day 4

Muktinath

Fire and water in the same place.

A short but exposed stretch through the wind-scoured Jomsom valley, past Kagbeni (the medieval gateway to the restricted zone). Climb up to Muktinath, one of the most sacred sites in the Himalayas: revered by both Hindus and Buddhists, home to 108 waterspouts and a natural gas flame burning above a spring.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 3,760 m
Night: Muktinath
Space to Pause

Evening at a shared sacred site. Natural anchor for a practice or teaching linked to the place.

Day 5

Syangboche

Into the restricted zone.

Back down through Kagbeni for the permit check. From here the restricted zone begins. Landscape turns lunar: eroded ochre badlands, wind-carved pillars, and tiny villages clustered around chortens. A technical riding day of switchbacks and gravel. The 4x4 makes the same crossings in more comfort. Overnight at Syangboche, a quiet teahouse settlement at the day's high point.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 3,800 m
Night: Syangboche
Space to Pause

Evening. Almost no light pollution; strong night sky.

Day 6

Lo Manthang

The walled city appears in a bowl of high desert.

Over Nyi La pass, then through Geling and Ghami (home to the longest mani wall in Nepal) and Tsarang (former royal seat with a ruined five-storey palace). The walled city of Lo Manthang appears suddenly in a bowl of high desert. Park the bikes for three nights.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 3,840 m
Night: Lo Manthang
Space to Pause

Evening. Sunset walk along the walled city ramparts.

Day 7

Lo Manthang

Stay day.

Full day on foot inside the walled city. Visit the Royal Palace, Jampa Lhakhang (the God House with a three-storey Maitreya statue), Thubchen Gompa (assembly hall with 15th-century murals), and Chode Monastery (the working monastery of the city).

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 3,840 m
Night: Lo Manthang
Space to Pause

Full afternoon and evening. This is the longest continuous open window of the trip, and a natural place to anchor a longer teaching or shared practice.

Day 8

Chhoser

Day ride, second Lo Manthang night.

Half-day ride out to Chhoser to see the Jhong sky caves (a 50m honeycomb of over 40 hand-carved chambers, purpose unknown, believed to be 2,000 to 3,000 years old) and the nearby Nyphu and Garphu cave gompas, still used by monks for retreat. Optional early ride at sunrise to Mustang Golf Course. Back to Lo Manthang for a final evening.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 3,840 m
Night: Lo Manthang
Space to Pause

Afternoon and evening. Second full open window in Lo Manthang.

Day 9

Chhusang

Via Luri Gompa.

Dip south through the Puyung valley, a quieter, less visited corner of Mustang. Leave the bikes at the Ghara trailhead for a short uphill hike (about 45 minutes) to Luri Gompa: the oldest monastery in the region, a cave temple with 700-year-old murals essentially undisturbed. Continue down through Tetang to Chhusang for the night.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 2,980 m
Night: Chhusang
Space to Pause

The visit to Luri Gompa itself is quiet. The site does most of the work.

Day 10

Kagbeni

Leaving the restricted zone.

Short, easy ride out of the restricted zone. Permits stamped on exit at Kagbeni. Whole afternoon free to explore the medieval alleyways and sit by the river confluence as the afternoon wind picks up.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 2,800 m
Night: Kagbeni
Space to Pause

Full afternoon and evening.

Day 11

Lete

Into the pine forests.

Back through Jomsom and Marpha, then on into pine and rhododendron forest at Lete. The first proper oxygen-rich stop after eight days at altitude.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 2,480 m
Night: Lete
Space to Pause

Afternoon and evening. Good integration day before returning to the city.

Day 12

Pokhara

Descent and hot springs.

Final ride down through Ghasa, where Annapurna I and Dhaulagiri frame the valley from either side, to Tatopani for a soak in the natural hot springs. Back in Pokhara by evening. Closing dinner with the whole group.

Stats / Highlights
Alt: 800 m
Night: Pokhara
Space to Pause

Closing evening.

Day 13

Departure

Journey ends.

Bikes handed back over breakfast. Transfers to onward flights or road journeys. Trip ends: Pokhara

Stats / Highlights
Trip: Expedition Concludes
Space to Pause

Departure.
